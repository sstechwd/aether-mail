# Realistic income — Aether Mail

Not a pitch deck. What can actually pay rent.

## The only model that fits

**Free MIT client. Money = Aether+ (optional hosted models + later conveniences).**  
Same shape as Hermes / Nous Portal. People who already pay OpenAI keep BYOK. People who hate keys pay us one bill. Mail always works offline of us.

We do **not** sell the `.exe`. Thunderbird is free. A $29 store app dies.

## Honest numbers (order of magnitude)

Assume Aether+ is **$12/mo** with a token allotment we buy from OpenRouter at roughly **40–60% of list**. Gross after model cost ~$5–7/seat if they stay in-allotment. Power users who blow the cap still pay us overage; we keep a thin margin.

| Paying seats | Gross / mo (approx) | After model + cards (~35%) |
|---|---|---|
| 20 friends | $240 | ~$80 — hobby |
| 150 indie privacy folks | $1,800 | ~$700 — part-time |
| 800 | $9,600 | ~$3,500 — one person salary-ish |
| 5,000 | $60k | you need support + a real entity |

Getting to 150 paying users is **years of a trusted daily driver**, not a weekend launch. Superhuman’s price only works because they sold taste to finance people. We sell *not uploading the mailbox*.

## Path that is not fantasy

1. Private daily-driver on your own mail (now).
2. 10 people you know, source build, no charge.
3. Public MIT + “Aether+ optional” when IMAP is boringly reliable.
4. YouTube / blog: **show the local agent on real mail**, not a landing page. Content finds BYOK users; a fraction upgrade because they don’t want to manage keys.
5. OpenRouter reseller account + Stripe. No GPU farm.

## What will not make money

- Charging for send/search.
- Ads.
- Selling the company after a demo video.
- A mobile store app in year one (Apple cut + review + OAuth hell).

## Mobile money

Phone in year one = **the same local UI in a browser**, later a Tauri/PWA wrapper. Not a third subscription. Aether+ is the same account.

## When to spend money

Do not incorporate or buy ads until 20 people have used Aether+ for a month without refunding. Until then, time is the only investment.
